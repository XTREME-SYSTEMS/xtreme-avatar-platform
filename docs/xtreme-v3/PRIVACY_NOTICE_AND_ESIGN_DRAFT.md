## Plain-language key points

1. You control what you share and which optional capabilities you activate.
2. The system may use the information you supply to personalize the assistant and carry out your own requests.
3. Proposed company promise: **we do not sell personal information.** This must be reconciled with actual vendor contracts, advertising pixels, analytics and local law before publication.
4. Your connected tools, website content, phone calls, cloud-browser sessions, and agent actions need separate authorizations. You can revoke an optional connector without deleting your whole account.
5. Generalized model training from identifiable customer content is **optional, off by default**. Declining it does not turn off essential services.
6. A longer privacy notice does not substitute for concise, separate affirmative consent for sensitive or optional data use.

## 1. Identity of service operator and scope

This notice applies to the Xtreme Avatar AI platform and associated XPS Intelligence experiences only after the verified operator has adopted it and provided an effective date. It covers both a consumer's personal settings and a user's authorized business workspace. It must not be confused with the privacy terms of Google, Tavus, Twilio, third-party websites or independent connected services. Those providers remain subject to their own disclosures.

## 2. Categories of collected information

Identity and contact information: chosen account identity, full name, primary and optional secondary phone number, and the verified account email. Professional information: website URL, company/industry, job title, business stage, service needs and intended use. Personalization: three wishes, goals, autonomy selection, preferred assistance level, topic preferences, skin, personality and voice choices. Operational content: user instructions, documents, media, chat or voice transcripts when enabled, progress and approval records, installed packs, workflow identifiers and result receipts. Diagnostics: app version, device capabilities, error codes and scoped network usage. Connected services: only the fields and actions expressly authorized for each provider. Browser data: cookies, screenshots, pages, request metadata and form content only when a browser session is explicitly enabled.

Sensitive information, precise location, biometric/face source media and government identifiers should not be requested without a separate purpose, specific disclosure and appropriate legal/technical safeguards.

## 3. Purposes of use and legal basis

We propose to use service information to authenticate accounts, deliver requested agent capabilities, remember preferences, adapt user-visible recommendations, provide source-backed results, maintain security, fulfill lawful obligations and debug reliability. Any use for advertising, third-party enrichment, sharing with unknown recipients, generalized AI training, or secondary analytics must be disclosed separately and enabled only when lawful. Service personalization may depend on selected memory, but the user should have meaningful granular controls.

## 4. Data sales, sharing and subprocessors

No sale of personal information is the intended contractual product policy. Limited disclosure to infrastructure, hosting, support, payment, model, communications, authentication, avatar or other vendors may be necessary to provide requested services. Before release, publish an accurate subprocessors list, geographic transfer mechanisms, processor restrictions, category-specific practices, and a method to object or exercise legally required privacy choices. Never make a universal 'we never share' claim when provider processing is necessary.

## 5. AI instructions and model improvement

A request may be processed by approved AI providers to produce an answer or perform a user-authorized workflow. This is different from retaining data for training an underlying general model. Optional customer-content model training must use a separate, unchecked opt-in and an accessible withdrawal mechanism. No personal note, conversation, contact list, employer directory, or proprietary business document should be exported for generalized improvement merely because a user signed the Terms of Service.

## 6. Memory, personalization and profiling

Allow users to review, edit, export, limit and delete retained memory where technically and legally possible. Keep personal, organization, team and public knowledge permission domains separate. Inferences and recommendations are fallible, not guarantees. Automations producing legal, employment, credit, insurance, medical or major financial consequences require specialized workflows and safeguards. Do not use the service to make regulated eligibility decisions without an approved regulatory basis.

## 7. Cloud Browser session privacy

Cloud Browser is optional. Explicitly explain the domain scope, actions permitted, whether the user must stay present, how session secrets and cookies are protected, what screenshots or history are retained, the remote browser operator and the process to revoke access. A provider's API key is stored only in a server-side secret store. CAPTCHA or anti-bot challenges must be honored: the system pauses and asks the user to resolve them directly or uses a site-authorized integration where lawful. Do not treat connection as authorization to defeat website restrictions.

## 8. Data enrichment and skip tracing

Data enrichment is not enabled by onboarding alone. If offered, it must specify the vendor, use case, lawful basis, source category, accuracy and correction mechanism, retention, sharing limits and user consent/approval. Do not use open-ended third-party person-locating searches without a validated lawful purpose. If a service supplies consumer reports or employment, tenant, credit, insurance or similar regulated screening data, applicable FCRA obligations and permissible-purpose requirements must be satisfied before use. Mere user consent is not always sufficient.

## 9. Communications, recording and marketing

Separate account service notifications from marketing communications. Obtain independent opt-ins where required for automated calls, recording, SMS/WhatsApp and other marketing outreach. Honor opt-outs, do-not-call restrictions, recipient consent, rate limits and channel-specific evidence. AI participation in live calls should be disclosed to participants as required. Sending a form cannot authorize contacting people named by the user without their own lawful contact basis.

## 10. Avatar and voice

Explain whether users provide their own likeness, whether a provider creates a synthetic face/voice, how consent to train or use a likeness is obtained, when sessions are recorded or transcribed, how long media is stored, and how to revoke or delete it. Prevent impersonation, unauthorized biometric collection and leakage between tenants. A demo avatar image is not a live recording or working video service.

## 11. Security, access and retention

Use least privilege, authentication, purpose-scoped OAuth, secret rotation, encryption in transit and at rest, tenant RLS, audit trails, rate limits and backups. Retention should be as short as reasonably necessary for the purpose, with explicit distinct schedules for onboarding fields, voice/video media, private files, browser traces, model logs, payment records and legally required signing receipts. Publish actual retention windows after checking each provider, not unsupported promises.

## 12. Individual rights

Provide a privacy request process for access, confirmation, correction, deletion, portability, consent revocation, marketing opt-out and appeal where required. Verify requesters without collecting excessive additional data. Include state, country and sector-specific disclosures if applicable. Access to deletion, correction and privacy choices should remain available without speaking to a sales representative.

## 13. Children and vulnerable users

The service should default to adults unless an intentionally designed and lawfully compliant youth experience is approved. Do not solicit information about minors or third-party sensitive characteristics in general onboarding. Protect information about family members from improper team sharing.

## 14. Automated decision-making and user control

Full Auto means bounded user-authorized reversible work. It is not unrestricted permission to spend, sign, publish, message, buy, delete or access unrelated accounts. Material irreversible actions, financial transactions, sensitive communications and third-party rights require scoped approvals and receipts. A user can switch modes or revoke permissions independently.

## 15. Electronic records and signatures

A production electronic signature should identify the exact agreement version/hash, signer identity and verification method, timestamp, affirmative separate checkboxes, method of signing, audit events, immutable record, exportable signed copy and any required consumer e-delivery notices and withdrawal method. The service must clearly distinguish acknowledgment of privacy disclosures from any optional sensitive processing consent. Draft signature controls in a preview are not actual binding agreements.

## 16. Changes, incidents and contacts

Material policy changes must be disclosed before secondary data uses begin, with renewed consent where required. Explain incident notification procedures to the extent applicable, identify how to reach the privacy team, and provide a current version archive. **Proposed consent controls**

- Required: "I acknowledge that I have reviewed the Privacy Notice and agree to the processing necessary to provide the services I select." This is not a blanket consent to all future uses.
- Optional, unchecked: "I allow my identifiable prompts and feedback to be used for generalized model improvement, under the linked model-improvement notice."
- Optional, unchecked: "I allow business-enrichment review for the specific purpose I select after reviewing the vendor and data-source disclosure."
- Optional, unchecked: "I want to connect a scoped Cloud Browser after approving its domains and actions."
- Optional, unchecked: "I consent to non-essential promotional communications under separate channel-specific terms."
- Typed signature: full name, with a server-verified, versioned e-sign audit receipt. Provide a copy to the user.
